# Welcome to my website

Hello! Let me give you a small introduction to this place. This website is
basically a brain dump of all the stuff I know about anything with no particular
order, no correctness and no sense. There's this concept in the world wide web
called
[digital garden](https://publish.obsidian.md/hub/05+-+Concepts/A+Brief+History+and+Ethos+of+the+Digital+Garden)
and I am making one of those right here right now.

What is that you mean? Well, basically a Digital Garden is:

> A garden is a collection of evolving ideas that aren't strictly organised by
> their publication date. They're inherently exploratory – notes are linked
> through contextual associations. They aren't refined or complete - notes are
> published as half-finished thoughts that will grow and evolve over time.
> They're less rigid, less performative, and less perfect than the personal
> websites we're used to seeing.

## My little digital garden

I started this project after getting stuck with my previous website attempts. I
tried quite hard to create a beautiful eye-catching website with amazing fonts
and animations. I really thought that it was going to be easy to create one of
those jaw-dropping websites like
[Patrick's incredible portfolio](https://bepatrickdavid.com/) in one month. I
was really wrong.

You need to know one thing about be: Professionally, I am a fullstack engineer.
But at heart, I'll always be a backend engineer.

I know CSS and HTML, of course. But I don't know how to make visually stunning
effects with that. I made an ERP from scratch back in 2020, but that required
only some HTML forms and a lot of backend code. No animations, no beautiful
fonts, no nothing. It was fun tho.

Here I crafted a lovely search bar with some posts. You can search for anything
you want in there. I indexed the posts titles AND contents. Because of that, you
are able to make a full-text search through my knowledge network. You are
welcome, btw.

## My current stack

So, my current tech stack is not anything crazy or complicated. I am quite
experienced with React and NodeJs servers, so I wanted to try out something new.
Because of that, I actually picked this stack:

- Rust and Actix. Really, two amazing pieces of software. Check out
  [Rust](coding/rust) or [Actix](https://actix.rs/)
- HTMX just so all HTML is server-side generated. No Javascript framework for a
  pet project. More HTMX [in here](https://htmx.org/)
- TailwindCSS just because I wanted.

And that's it. No fancy libraries or frameworks. Simple, lightweight and fast.
