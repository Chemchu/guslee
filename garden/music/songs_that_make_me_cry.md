# Songs that make me cry

I love music. For the love of God I love music so fucking much. I love how it
tickles my brain and how it makes me feel. Thanks to the amount of convinience
we humans managed to create for ourselves, we have every single piece of media
we want in the palm of our hands.

So, these past few weeks I've been listening to old songs from media I've
consumed throughout the years. I don't know what's wrong with me but honestly
sometimes I get to listen to an old song and, for whatever reason it might be, I
start fucking crying. So, this post is about that. Here are three songs that
make me cry

## Hopes And Dreams from Undertale (by Toby Fox)

Honestly, the whole OST is amazing. Toby Fox is just a genius. Dude was able to
create a really cool and original game filled with funny moments and lovable
characters. Credits where its due, if that wasn't impressive enough, the guys
also created a masterpiece of an original soundtrack. All of the songs in
Undertale are a banger, but playing for the first time the pacifist route and
witnessing that finale while also listening to the most amazing piece of music
ever, that's just incredible. The combo of "Hopes And Dreams" + "SAVE the
World" + "His Theme" is just a punch to my mental health.

If you are curious, this is
[the song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wbO3p7_Mf30)

## Hiroshi No Kaisou from a Shin Chan movie

So, there's this old movie called "Shin Chan: The Adult Empire Strikes Back"
from 2001. If you are not familiar with Shin Chan, I am sorry. It truly is an
incredible piece of japanese culture. Lucky me when I moved from Brazil to Spain
in 2010. The spanish public television have a great amount of old cartoons and
movies. Just so happens that I got to grow up watching Shin Chan on TV. Just as
a brief context filler for you my friend, Shin Chan is this really old (and
ongoing) TV animated cartoon about a 5yo japanese boy and his family. Each
episode is about how Shin Chan and his family interact with people in a funny
and chaotic way. The story covers pretty much every single possible situation
the writers desire. From "Shin Chan missing his school bus" to "the Nohara
family saving the world while singing a song while punching a russian looking
guy who has the power to read mind".

So, back to the topic of this post, the music. This song is from a movie where
Shinnosuke's father and mother get brainwashed into thinking they are kids
again. Trying to avoid spoilers here but we get to see Shinnosuke's dad past and
how his life was until that point. All of this with an amazing melancolic and
emotional song in the background. This is the peak emotional point of the movie
and it really is a heartbreaking one. I fucking love this movie and
[this song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zeqyKrTZiKo)

## 14.3 Billion Years from Outer Wilds

HOLY SHIT OUTER WILDS MENTIONED! Yes, best fucking game ever for the love of
FUCKING GOD. So, Outer Wilds. I don't know where to begin honestly. Outer Wilds
is a game released way back in 2019 where you play as a goofy little alien
exploring a miniature solar system. There's no clear main objective in the game.
The only thing you know is that, for some reason, you are unable to die. Every
time you die, you wake up in the same place in the same moment in time. The game
is about exploring this solar system and uncovering the mysteries it hides. This
game talks about very deep themes like love, death and learning to let go. It
truly is a game that resonates so much with its player base because of the story
and because of the music.

After you beat the game, you get to see the credits! Yay! And that's where this
song hits the hardest. Throughout the game, you'll be listening to same
leitmotiv over and over again. It's a nice beautiful one, but it really hits
hard when the credits rolls and you get to hear it again for the last time with
all of the loud and amazing orchesta. I love this game and I love this song. I
can't help but cry every single time I listen to it. This is
[the song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XOrygf_iLhw) but please don't listen
to it. Just play the game and you'll get what I mean

Well, that's it. See you around!
